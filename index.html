ABOUT ME

From grassroots experience to global engagement.

My journey began in a rural community in West Arsi, Ethiopia, where I experienced firsthand the challenges faced by young people growing up with limited access to education, infrastructure, information and opportunities.

Those experiences shaped my commitment to ensuring that young people, especially those whose voices are often underrepresented, have meaningful opportunities to participate in leadership, policy and development.

Today, my work connects youth leadership, ethical governance, diplomacy, peacebuilding, civic participation and sustainable development.

I believe that young people should not only be consulted about the future. They should help shape it.
